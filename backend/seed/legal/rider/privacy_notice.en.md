## 2. The data we collect

**Data you give us**

- Your phone number, name and, if you add them, your e-mail address and photo.

**Data created when you use the app**

- Pickup, stops and drop-off points, the route, times, distance, fare, payment method and the status of each trip.

**Data from others**

- From drivers: ratings and comments about a trip, and reports about lost items or incidents.
- From payment providers: whether a payment succeeded.
- From people who book for you or send you a package: your name and phone number.

## 3. Why we use your data and our legal bases

- **To provide the service** (performing our contract with you): to create your account, show prices, find a driver, run the trip, take payment and issue receipts.
- **For safety and security** (our legitimate interests and, where it applies, your vital interests): to verify accounts, detect fraud such as fake bookings or false GPS, investigate incidents and respond to emergencies.
- **To support you** (contract and legitimate interests): to answer questions, resolve complaints and return lost items.
- **To improve the service** (legitimate interests): to understand demand, set fair prices and fix problems, using aggregated data where we can.
- **To keep records and meet the law** (legal obligation): tax and accounting records, and requests from authorities, including the transport regulator {{regulator}} where the law requires trip reporting.
- **To tell you about offers** (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the app or by contacting us).

## 4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

- **Your driver**: your first name, rating, pickup and drop-off points and, during the trip, your location and the messages you send. Calls and messages go through the app where possible so your number stays private.
- **People you choose**: trusted contacts and anyone you share a trip with see the trip and the driver's details while the share is active.
- **Payment providers**: to take and refund payments.
- **Service providers** who host our systems, send text messages and notifications, provide maps and routing, and help with support. They act on our instructions and must protect your data.
- **Authorities**: the police, courts, tax authorities and the transport regulator {{regulator}} when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.
- **A buyer or successor** if our business is sold or reorganised; this notice will continue to apply.

## 5. Location data

We use your precise location to set your pickup point, show nearby drivers and follow the trip. We collect it while the app is in use for booking or a trip. You can turn location permission off in your phone's settings; you can then type an address, but some features will not work.

We keep data only as long as we need it:

- Messages with drivers, for a limited period after the trip.
- Sign-in codes and sessions, for a short period.
- Safety and fraud records, as long as needed to investigate and to defend legal claims.

When a period ends we delete the data or make it anonymous.
