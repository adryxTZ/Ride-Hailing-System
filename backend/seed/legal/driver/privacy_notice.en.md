## 2. The data we collect

**While you drive**

**From others**

## 3. Why we use your data and our legal bases

- **To decide your application and keep you eligible** (contract and legal obligation): checking identity, licence, vehicle and insurance, and reminding you before documents expire.
- **To offer you rides and run trips** (contract): matching you with nearby riders, navigation, fares, cash and in-app payments.
- **To pay you and keep accounts** (contract and legal obligation): earnings, the platform's share, debts, payouts, receipts, tax and accounting records.
- **For safety and fraud prevention** (legitimate interests and legal obligation): detecting false GPS, self-booking and cancellation abuse, investigating incidents, and responding to SOS alerts. A staff member reviews any flag before action is taken; we do not punish anyone on an automated signal alone.
- **To meet regulation** (legal obligation): registering you and your vehicle and reporting trips to {{regulator}} where the law requires it.
- **To support and improve the service** (legitimate interests).

## 4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data.

- **Riders** see your first name, photo, rating, vehicle make, model, colour and plate, and your location while you are on the way and during the trip.
- **Trusted contacts of a rider** who shares a trip see the same trip details while the share is active.
- **The transport regulator {{regulator}} and other authorities**, when the law requires it.
- **Payment providers** for pay-ins and payouts.
- **Service providers** for hosting, messages, maps, document checks and support, on our instructions.
- **A buyer or successor** if our business is sold or reorganised.
