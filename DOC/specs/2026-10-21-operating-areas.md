# Spec: operating areas (countries and regions marked on the map, scoping prices and dashboards)

Factory run started 2026-10-21. Spec by the spec-writer agent (condensed here; defaults in §9 apply until the owner says otherwise). Owner's words: multiple currencies; operate in several countries, each divided into regions/counties/states chosen by the admin; different reports and dashboards per country; "I don't see region marking on admin".

## 1. Goal
A brand can run in several countries. Each country has its own currency, timezone, tax and levy policy, regulator and payment gateway, and is divided into **areas** (named per country: Region/Mkoa, County/Kaunti, State) that staff mark on the admin map by clicking H3 cells or drawing a polygon. A quote takes its area from the pickup and is priced with that area's tariff, fee policy, fixed fares and surge cap (country defaults when the area has none), in the country's currency, never converted. Trips store country and area. Overview, Live, Trips, Pricing and Finance follow a country/area picker in the side nav. Drivers register in areas of one country, get offers only there, see their areas on Home. Tanzania ships ACTIVE with Dar es Salaam marked; a second country (Kenya) is set up DRAFT.

## 2. Scope
**In:** countries as brand data (`brands/<code>.json` `countries[]` → `countries` table, read-only in admin); `internal/areas` module (areas, res-7 cell sets, in-process cell index, admin CRUD with audit); Areas page under Operate (list, create/edit, paint/erase cells, draw polygon, save with reason; other areas' cells locked); area outlines on the Live map; tariffs, fee policies, fixed routes scoped to country + optional area (area first, then country); per-area surge on/off and cap; quotes resolve the area from the pickup (pickups outside every ACTIVE area refused; cross-border drop-off refused); `quotes`/`trips` store `country_code`, `area_id`; splits use the trip area's policy, debt limits the driver's country policy; admin scope picker (country required, area optional) scoping Overview, Live, Trips, Pricing, Finance (country only), midnight from the country timezone; `driver_profiles.country_code` + `driver_areas` (1 primary + up to 4), onboarding asks for the area, staff edit areas, dispatch offers only inside the driver's areas, going online outside them refused, Home shows areas chip and "outside your areas" notice; `/app/config` lists ACTIVE countries.
**Out:** currency conversion and cross-country consolidated reports; editing countries in admin; multiple gateways/regulators at once; area-level ledger accounts; drivers changing their own areas; cells finer than res 7 or shapefile imports; SOS scoping; area-based dispatch tuning.

## 3. Contract (summary)
Schemas `Country`, `Area {id, country, code, name, status ACTIVE|DRAFT|ARCHIVED, surge_enabled, surge_max?, cell_count, cells (compacted H3 ≤ res 7), bbox, updated_at}`, `AreaRef`, `Scope {country, area_id?, currency, timezone}`.
Routes: `GET /admin/countries`; `GET /admin/areas?country=`; `POST /admin/areas` (Operations); `PATCH /admin/areas/{id}` (status rules: `COUNTRY_NOT_ACTIVE`, `AREA_NOT_READY {missing}`, `AREA_HAS_DRIVERS`, `AREA_HAS_ACTIVE_TRIPS`); `PUT /admin/areas/{id}/cells` (`AREA_CELL_INVALID`, `AREA_CELL_RESOLUTION`, `AREA_TOO_LARGE`, `AREA_CELLS_OVERLAP {cells, areas}`, `AREA_ARCHIVED`); `PUT /admin/drivers/{id}/areas` (Operations, Driver Review). Scope query `country`, `area_id` on overview/live/trips/pricing/drivers; finance by country; responses add `scope`; `AdminLive.areas`; `AdminTrip.country/area`. Pricing writes take `country` + optional `area_id`; `DELETE /admin/pricing/tariffs/{service}/{class}` removes an area override (`COUNTRY_DEFAULT_REQUIRED`); `TariffView.inherited`; area policies may not change tax/debt fields (`POLICY_FIELD_COUNTRY_ONLY`); fixed routes get `country`, `area_id` (`CURRENCY_MISMATCH`, `ROUTE_ORIGIN_OUTSIDE_AREA`). `POST /quotes`: `Quote.area`, errors `OUTSIDE_SERVICE_AREA`, `CROSS_BORDER_NOT_SUPPORTED`. Driver: onboarding `area_options`/`area_id` (`AREA_REQUIRED`), status `areas`, `current_area`, `in_registered_area`, online → `OUTSIDE_REGISTERED_AREA`. `/app/config.countries`.

## 4. Data
Migration `00028_operating_areas.sql`: `countries (brand_id, code PK, name jsonb, currency, timezone, area_term jsonb, regulator, payment_gateway, emergency_phone, map_lat/lng/zoom, status)`; `areas (id, brand_id, country_code, code, name, status, surge_enabled, surge_max)`; `area_cells (brand_id, cell bigint res-7, area_id) PK (brand_id, cell)` (the DB refuses overlap); `driver_areas (driver_id, area_id, is_primary)`; `driver_profiles.country_code` (backfilled); `tariffs` + `fee_policies` + `fixed_routes` + `quotes` + `trips` gain `country_code`/`area_id` with `UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT` scoping and indexes; config version scope `areas`. Brand file: `countries[]` (legacy single-country brand builds one entry; home country must be listed; validation fails fast). Seeds `seed/areas/TZ.json` (dar-es-salaam ACTIVE polygon, pwani DRAFT), `KE.json` (nairobi DRAFT); seed converts polygons with `h3.PolygonToCells(…, 7)` and never overwrites staff cells; pricing seeds gain `"country": "TZ"`. Redis: presence hash gains `areas`. Caches: in-process, versioned by `areas`. Startup refuses an ACTIVE country whose gateway or regulator does not match the configured adapters.

## 5. Behaviour
Constants: `Resolution 7`, `MaxCellsPerArea 200000`, `MaxCellsPerRequest 50000`, `OverlapDetailLimit 100`, `CacheTTL 5s`, `MaxDriverAreas 5`. Module `internal/areas/{doc,model,index,store,service,ports}.go`; `kernel/scope`. Resolve = res-7 cell map lookup; area and country must be ACTIVE; never a default area. Quote: pickup area first; other-country stops refused; tariff area→country→unavailable; policy area→country; fixed routes area or country (area wins at equal priority); surge off or capped per area; currency = country's; quote stores country/area; booking copies them. Policy cascade: a new country policy republishes every area policy with the tax/debt fields copied (audited). Area lifecycle DRAFT→ACTIVE (needs cells, policy, tariff) → ARCHIVED (cells released; refused with drivers or open trips). Overlap under an advisory lock, PK as the final guard. Drivers: onboarding area required; approval sets primary; eligibility carries areas; presence stores them; `Nearby` filters by area; online outside areas refused; staff edits rewrite presence. Dashboards: `Stats(ctx, since, scope)`, midnight per country; Live filtered by area; Finance by currency. Places search limited to ACTIVE country codes. Performance: resolve O(1) in process, 0 extra queries per quote; scoped stats on new indexes (EXPLAIN < 10 ms recorded).

## 6. Apps and admin
Admin: `ScopePicker` in the Sidebar (URL + localStorage), map centre from `Country.map` (no literal centre); Areas page (`features/areas/{AreasScreen,AreaList,AreaForm,AreaMapEditor,hooks,useCellPainter,usePolygonDraw}`), shared `components/map/areaLayers.ts`; Live shows outlines and fits the scoped area; Pricing shows "Country price"/"Set price for this {area}"; Drivers get an Areas editor. Driver app: onboarding `AreaPicker`, Home `AreasChip` + outside-area banner, 409 copy with area names. Rider: server messages only. EN/SW copy tables as in the full spec (nav.areas "Areas/Maeneo", `OUTSIDE_SERVICE_AREA` "We don't operate here yet. / Bado hatutoi huduma eneo hili.", etc.).

## 9. Owner questions (defaults in use)
1. Second country: Kenya (KES, County/Kaunti) kept DRAFT as the example; which country is really next?
2. Launch regions in Tanzania: Dar es Salaam live, Pwani draft; others (Arusha, Mwanza, Dodoma) at pilot?
3. Pickups outside every marked area are refused (no service in unmarked places).
4. Per-region: tariffs, commission, booking fee, waiting cap, rounding, cancellation terms; country-wide: VAT, LATRA levy, debt limits.
5. Trips crossing regions are priced and taxed by the pickup region.
6. Drivers: up to 5 areas in one country, chosen at sign-up, changed only by staff; online outside them refused.
7. Border accuracy ≈ 1.2 km hexagons is acceptable for pricing and reporting.
